Verification of the revised Jellium–UEG proof

Scope

Jellium.Main.equality proves equality at every positive hbar and density,
and Main.coupling_equality gives the equivalent positive-coupling statement.
Both invoke Conjecture51.equality, which proves the two comparisons from
explicit literature, operator and analytic laws on one ChargeModel and
StateAPI. The constrained indirect energy and canonical ball energy are
infima in that abstract model. Neither desired comparison nor the canonical
completion estimate is assumed as an input.

This is relative formal verification. Concrete trace-class Fock operators,
Sobolev domains, literature theorems and the local orbital-sampling construction
remain inputs. The lower confined-state predicate and upper near-ground-ball
law are separately supplied interfaces. Their interpretation as the same
physical canonical Dirichlet problem is not constructed from operator theory.
LITERATURE.txt describes these qualifications and the primary references.

Manuscript

The reviewed source is jellium_ueg.tex dated 12 October 2026. The update covers
the main proof in Section 4 and the shell completion in Appendix B, together
with the scaling reduction of the main theorem. The Basic facts subsection
is outside the requested formalization scope. Its analytic and operator
results remain explicit inputs. Existing helper identities are retained.
Appendix A reviews the literature behind the Jellium inputs and is not
separately rebuilt. verification.json records the precise manuscript and
local-source SHA-256 hashes.

Changes made for this revision

Main.UnitParameterInputs collects the existing lower, upper, localization
and interpolation inputs at hbar = 1. Main.equality uses these at each
positive density, invokes Conjecture51.equality, derives the powers hbar^6
and hbar^(-8) from density scaling, and proves the full parameter statement.
Main.coupling_equality derives the equivalent lambda formulation. The audit
now checks this connected proof, including all three scaling steps.
The intended finite spin multiplicity is represented by the selected state
model and constants, not by a constructed family of spin operators.

Neutralization.select_orbitals selects a realization at or below the exact
mean, retains the negative omitted self-interaction term, and no longer
adds an artificial unit error. The completion proof uses the shell estimate
at the outer radius directly, with the manuscript's excess-background sign
convention. The favorable negative charge-square term is retained before
the weaker completion result drops it for the lower comparison.

UniformCounting now proves pointwise counting bounds for the actual selected
periodic balls, uniformly over the translation cell. UpperBox uses these to
prove an explicit normalized energy remainder and the eventual uniform
upper bound in eq:attack_fixed_packing_upper. The existing equality proof
continues to use exact averaged coverage.

Proof map

1. General inputs and the bounded transition layer

The identity eq:conj_identity is CoulombForm.jellium_eq_indirect_add_charge,
specialized to the lower background by LowerPhysical.jellium_identity.
TrialState.StateAPI.product supplies the disjoint fermionic product law.
Its indirect-energy and Jellium-energy consequences are derived algebraically.
The Coulomb interpolation inequality, LO and HO inequalities, Newton/polar
laws and LLS localization remain named analytic or literature inputs.

Spatial and Boundary.local_sum_from_tiling derive finite-window cell counts,
boundary support, the partition-power estimate and the uniform local-energy
sum. The absolute cell energy controls missing interior volume for either
energy sign. Boundary.averaged_localization_lower_bound inserts that bound
into LLS localization. bounded_layer_from_standard_localization proves the
ordered large-box then large-cell limits. It requires no uniform kinetic
bound for the original density sequence.

2. Lower comparison and shell completion

InnerDensity constructs a smoothed inner-ball density with strict Dirichlet
support margin. It proves mass, pointwise bounds, both UEG scale conditions,
and vanishing normalized Coulomb discrepancy. The formal sequence has inner
radius (n+1)^2, width 1/(n+1), and confining radius (n+1)^2 + 2*A/(n+1).
This is an equivalent explicit choice rather than the exact ell^(-1/2)
parametrization in the manuscript.

LowerPhysical.exists_trial_sequence chooses constrained-search near-minimizers.
exists_sector_le selects an occupied sector with energy no greater than its
countable mixture average. It assumes neither integer mean particle number
nor radiality of the selected sector.

Neutralization.charge_bound derives the surface-order charge bound from the
Onsager/capacity lower bound and extensive upper energy estimate.
NeutralizationGeometry.exists_prepared_annulus constructs the rounded neutral
outer radius, positive integer shell population, fixed width and a lower
bound for the trimmed sampling volume. shellCharge_properties constructs the
radial signed shell charge and controls its mass and total variation.

NeutralizationElectrostatics proves shell cancellation through a symmetric
radial kernel estimate. This is an alternative to the manuscript's
cumulative-charge integration-by-parts identity eq:completion_shell_identity.
The outer-radius consequence is used directly by
Neutralization.shell_correction_bound_at_outer_radius. Its negative monopole
term is retained in exists_neutral_completion_with_monopole before being
dropped for the thermodynamic comparison.

OrbitalSample provides the iid classical expectation with its negative
omitted self-interaction, an integrable kinetic penalty, particle count and
local orbital energy estimate. select_orbitals proves favorable realization
selection. The continuum iid measure, actual orbitals, exact numerical
penalty constants and their Fock realization are not implemented here.
The completion theorem combines these local inputs with the proved charge,
geometry and cancellation estimates. LowerPhysical.lower_comparison then
uses only the canonical LN limit on neutral balls to obtain eJ <= eU.

3. Upper comparison

SmoothCollar and RadialCollar construct compensated profiles and prove their
neutrality, regularity, zero exterior potential and surface costs. Gluing,
FluidRegularity and UpperConstruction produce the core-plus-fluid geometry
and finite trial estimate. The fluid law is a bounded-density quasifree bound.
It suffices for the proved fluid density cap, but does not verify the full
unbounded-density LLS epsilon-dependent lemma or its numerical constants.

Packing and PackingParameters construct separated integer-neutral cores.
Lattice, PeriodicTrial, SelectedConstruction and PeriodicSelected construct
the measurable finite selection of translates. SmoothCube and UpperBackground
supply the fixed smoothed cubic background.

CoulombForm.core_fluid_identity and MeasurableTrial.given_product_jellium give
the exact fixed-background identity eq:attack_exact_upper_identity. Core-core,
core-collar and distinct-collar cross terms vanish by proved support and
Newton cancellation. No estimate for a moving outer background is used.

Periodic, Coverage, ProfileAverage, BaseProfiles, CenteredProfiles, JointDensity
and UpperDensity prove the averaged density plateau, bounded transition
layer and support. The exterior support is expressed through the containing
coordinate cube, sufficient for the same bounded-layer argument.
UpperDensity.average_uniform_bounds also proves the packing-independent cap
from core neutrality and coverage. The localization step uses the sufficient
fixed-packing cap, since the packing is held fixed in the large-box limit.
MixtureIntegrability, PotentialBound and UpperMixture
justify averaging at a fixed background. Only linear observables are affine.

UniformCounting compares two shifts in a fundamental cell and integrates
exact coverage at enlarged and reduced box sizes. It proves the actual
uniform counting error with constant 26*(radiusMax + 3*a + L), a larger
fixed-packing constant than the manuscript's 26*(a + L). The rate is still
O(R^2). UpperPhysical.fixed_packing_uniform_rate gives a normalized energy
remainder of order 1/R. fixed_packing_uniform_upper proves that, for every
positive eta, every shift is eventually bounded by the fixed-packing energy
plus eta. This is the epsilon form of eq:attack_fixed_packing_upper, without
introducing explicit supremum or limsup operators.

These uniform estimates supplement the equality proof, which uses the exact
averaged coverage from UpperCoverage and UpperEnergy. UpperBox and
UpperPhysical hold the packing fixed in the first limit, then send its error
to zero. Both versions handle either sign of eJ.

Retired arguments and audit

The old Canonical, CanonicalConstruction, Canonicalization,
ProbabilityApproximation and contraction-energy arguments remain historical
helpers. Generic mollifier and ball-volume helpers are still reused.
Audit.lean rejects use of retired assignment, contraction and scalar-obligation
proofs by the final equality. It also requires the main lower, shell, upper
and localization lemmas to occur in the equality's actual dependency graph.
The graph is rooted at Main.coupling_equality and also requires Main.equality,
Conjecture51.equality and all three EnergyScaling reductions.

The axiom audit covers every non-internal declaration imported under the
Jellium root, including its transitive dependencies.
Only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound are allowed. It rejects sorryAx
and other axioms, and prints the equality type and principal law structures.
This logical audit does not prove that the physical inputs are implemented.

Reproduction

From lean/ run

    python3 check.py

The current full run uses python3 check.py --jobs 4. This forces the same
checks with four compiler processes, scheduled after their local imports.

This forces a rebuild of all local sources with autoImplicit=false,
relaxedAutoImplicit=false and warnings treated as errors. For changed modules
and a fresh audit run

    python3 check.py --build Jellium.lean Audit.lean

The checker verifies the compiler version against lean-toolchain and always
reruns Audit.lean, including when its compiled output is current. Dependencies
use the existing mathlib cache. No dependency files are modified.
The cached mathlib commit matches the pinned revision. Its only tracked
differences are existing executable-bit changes on Python utility scripts,
with no content changes. The verification does not execute those scripts.

The unchanged runner was previously checked using a temporary compiler fixture. Those checks
covered dependency ordering with parallel workers, incremental freshness,
mandatory audit execution, wrong-version rejection and failure propagation.
